# Trademark policy

The code is licensed under Apache-2.0 and the documentation prose under CC BY 4.0.
Neither license grants rights to the **ContextOS** name or logo (Apache-2.0,
section 6). This policy says how you may use them.

## Allowed without asking

- Saying truthfully that your product uses, embeds or is built on ContextOS
  ("powered by ContextOS", "works with ContextOS").
- Referring to the project, its specification and this runtime in articles, talks,
  documentation and comparisons.
- Distributing unmodified releases under the ContextOS name.

## Needs written permission

- Using ContextOS in the name of your product, company, service or domain.
- Distributing a modified runtime under the ContextOS name. Rename your fork
  (for example "Acme Gateway, based on ContextOS").
- Using the logo, other than to link to the project.

## Never

- Claiming or implying that a product is ContextOS conformant or certified because it
  runs this code. Conformance comes from the spec's assessment of a deployed workflow,
  not from the name.
- Implying endorsement by, or affiliation with, the ContextOS project when there is
  none.

Questions and permission requests go to the maintainers (see
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)).
